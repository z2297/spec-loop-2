- `runSliceError` (the caught-exception record) calls `esc(...)` with **three explicit options**:

  ran.

  `taskBlockReason(r)` as the context. There is no branch on the last status: an exhausted
  genuine `BLOCKED` gets `ambiguity` exactly like an exhausted no-result.
- In `plugins/spec-loop/agents/slice-worker-fallback.md`, the no-result/trigger rules live under
  the `## Escalations` heading, NOT under Pipeline step 4 (step 4 is "Review ∥ quality gate").
